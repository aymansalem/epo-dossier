Coherent Evidentiary Chain

Coherent Evidentiary Chain — Historical Split-Stay Implementation The following exhibits document the Hotels Plus booking system’s architecture, configuration, executable relocation logic, booking parameters, and result presentation. The central evidence concerns the CORE split-stay mechanism: identifying successive accommodation portions when a requested stay cannot be fulfilled as one continuous accommodation arrangement, and constructing a combined stay involving room or hotel relocation. The remaining exhibits document supporting architecture, configuration, and additional booking functionality surrounding that mechanism. Their significance is assessed in context, rather than by requiring word-for-word correspondence with individual claim limitations. Read together with the Documentary and Visual annexes, the Tech Annex presents the implementation, corroborating records, and historical context of the Hotels Plus system. The Examining Division is invited to assess this evidence against the claimed CORE and the additional limitations in the relevant claim set.

Classification principle: Exhibits are identified as BASE / CORE, ADD-ON, or SUPPORTING according to their evidentiary relationship to the split-stay mechanism. ADD-ON features are assessed as additions to the Claim 1 CORE, not treated as independent inventions merely because they introduce further functionality. The evidence is intended to establish the historical implementation and context of the CORE; the patentability of additional claim limitations remains for assessment by the Examining Division.

  1. ANNEX-T-002, ANNEX-T-003, ANNEX-T-005 — The database architecture stores account-level and property-level records and relocation settings, supporting the historical implementation context of the Claim 1 CORE.
  2. ANNEX-T-004, ANNEX-T-007, ANNEX-T-008, ANNEX-T-011, ANNEX-T-014 — Relocation settings, account permissions, and room-quantity controls support the configuration and control surrounding the Claim 1 CORE.
  3. ANNEX-T-006, ANNEX-T-015 — Backend search logic and split-stay parameter construction provide the central technical evidence for the Claim 1 CORE: searching successive stay portions and constructing a combined stay through room or hotel relocation.
  4. ANNEX-T-009, ANNEX-T-013, ANNEX-T-016 — User-facing relocation disclosures, the availability-search demonstration, and result rendering support the search and presentation stages surrounding the Claim 1 CORE.
  5. ANNEX-T-010, ANNEX-T-018, ANNEX-T-019 — Property coordinates, geographic calculations, and distance-range filtering provide ADD-ON context relevant to Claim 12, which inherits the Claim 1 CORE. The evidence is not presented as independently proving a distance test between the first and second listings.
  6. ANNEX-T-020, ANNEX-T-021, ANNEX-T-022, ANNEX-T-023 — Customer records, booking history, payment, wishlist, and loyalty data provide contextual evidence relevant to Claim 13’s user-interaction limitation, assessed in the context of the Claim 1 CORE.
  7. ANNEX-T-012, ANNEX-T-024 — Booking records and reservation-timeout handling provide supporting transactional context; they are not presented as independent proof of the split-stay generation mechanism.
  8. ANNEX-T-017 — Configurable minimum-stay rules provide ADD-ON context relevant to Claim 3, which inherits the Claim 1 CORE. The specific five-day value is not asserted as a historically implemented Hotels Plus setting.
  9. ANNEX-T-025 — The annotated split-search sketch provides explanatory support for the Claim 1 CORE and its corresponding system and storage-device forms in Claims 14 and 15; it should be read alongside the executable-code and historical operational evidence.
